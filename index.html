A field guide · Redhill Close, Singapore

Ten plates of nasi lemak worth leaving home for.

A dedicationMade specially for the three craftlets — may every one of these plates be worth the drive.

Ten nasi lemak plates in Singapore's west and centre, every one of them within fifteen minutes of Redhill Close and — the part that took the most checking — actually open at the weekend. Ranked with a thumb on the scale for somewhere indoor to sit and a chicken curry worth ordering. Measured throughout against one reference plate, Cafe Sunset Railway.

◆The reference plate

Cafe Sunset Railway

41 Sunset Way · #01-02 Clementi Arcade · Thu–Sun only

Coconut basmati rice, chicken curry on the menu, a plate that arrives on ceramic.
Indoor cafe Chicken curry ✓ Sat–Sun 8am ✓

Confirmed still trading in 2026, four days a week, 8am to 3pm. The counterpoint on this list — not a hawker stall but a small, quiet neighbourhood cafe that has become the yardstick for a certain kind of considered, sit-down nasi lemak. Basmati grain, chicken curry listed alongside the rice, and a room you can sit in out of the heat. It happens to satisfy both of the newer preferences, which is part of why it works as a reference. Every other entry is measured against it.

Bibik Violet

Temasek Shophouse · 28 Orchard Road · #01-05

Chicken curry at $14.90, in a 54-seat air-conditioned room, seven days a week.
Indoor · aircon Chicken curry ✓ Sat–Sun 10:00–21:30

Still the only entry clearing every preference at once — indoor seating, a chicken curry listed in its own right, and open all seven days. Weekday breakfast starts at 8am; at weekends the doors open at 10am instead, so treat it as a late brunch rather than an early start. Confirmed trading in 2026.

vs. Sunset RailwayThe same pairing — nasi lemak and chicken curry in a cafe setting — but open seven days against the benchmark’s four. Sunset Railway answers back on portion size and on being the quieter room.

The Coconut Club

269 Beach Road · shophouse dining room · relocated

Bario rice, ayam goreng berempah, and a Michelin Bib Gourmand to its name.
Indoor · aircon Rendang, not curry chicken Weekends 11:00–22:30

Note the address: the Ann Siang Hill shophouse this guide previously listed has closed, and the restaurant now runs from 269 Beach Road with room for close to a hundred diners. Weekends are its best window — open straight through from 11am to 10:30pm with no afternoon break. Closed the first Monday of each month. The signature chicken is fried berempah, not curried.

vs. Sunset RailwayThe same idea with a bigger budget — both sit-down, ceramic-plate nasi lemak, but Coconut Club sources Bario coconuts from Sarawak and charges accordingly. Sunset Railway is the version you can justify weekly, and the one that actually serves chicken curry.

Kitchenman Nasi Lemak

2 Kallang Avenue · #01-08/13 CT Hub · closed Sundays

A Michelin Bib Gourmand plate with rendang chicken and ayam kunyit on the sides list.
Indoor shop unit Rendang & kunyit chicken Saturday only

New to this guide and the strongest arrival in a while: a Malaysian-run shop that opened in 2024 and took a Michelin Bib Gourmand for straightforward, properly made nasi lemak. Rice cooked in coconut milk with pandan, and a sides list that runs to ayam berempah, rendang chicken and turmeric-fried ayam kunyit. No pork or lard, though it is not halal-certified. The catch is Sunday — it trades Monday to Saturday only.

vs. Sunset RailwayThe closest thing on this list to a like-for-like challenger: a small kitchen, an indoor room, a curried chicken option, and a critic’s badge. Sunset Railway keeps the edge on Sunday opening and on being fifteen minutes nearer the west.

04City Gate

Uptown Nasi Lemak

371 Beach Road · #01-27 City Gate · branch switched

Damansara-style KL nasi lemak, in a mall unit that actually opens at weekends.
Indoor mall unit Curry unconfirmed Daily 11:00–20:00

The Telok Ayer branch this guide used to point at has gone weekday-only — Monday to Friday, closed both weekend days — which knocks it out on your rules. The City Gate outlet on Beach Road is the one to use instead: open Monday through Sunday, 11am to 8pm, in an air-conditioned mall unit. Same KL-style kitchen, longer hours, no last-Saturday-of-the-month catch.

vs. Sunset RailwayCovers every day the benchmark does not, indoors and with a longer evening. What it gives up is character — this is a mall counter, not a room you linger in over kopi.

Mr & Mrs Nasi Lemak

243 Cantonment Road · couple-run shop · closed Sundays

A cult couple-run plate with unusual toppings, from about $5.
Indoor shop unit Curry unconfirmed Saturday only

A small, well-loved operation that has moved twice and kept its following each time, now settled on Cantonment Road. Unusual add-ons and a plate that starts around $5. Two caveats: it is closed every Sunday, so it only half-satisfies the weekend rule, and the most recent independent write-up dates to 2024 — though it still lists live on Grab at 4.6, which is reasonable evidence it is trading.

vs. Sunset RailwaySimilar in spirit — a small kitchen with a point of view and a room to sit in — but Saturday-only against the benchmark’s Thursday-to-Sunday. Cheaper, scrappier, and the sambal has more front to it.

Crispy wings and fried bee hoon — the breakfast stall on your own doorstep.

The nearest entry by a wide margin and the only one open all seven days without qualification: 6am to 12:30pm and again 5:30 to 8:30pm Monday through Saturday, and 6am to 2pm on Sunday. Reviews through May 2026 confirm it is still going. The crispy chicken wing is what the queue is for.

Lontong and nasi lemak from a stall that opens at half past five in the morning.

The Malay stall at ABC Brickworks, four minutes away, trading from 5:30am to 1:30pm. Called Singapore’s best-rated nasi lemak, in the prettiest market building in town.

22 Clementi Road · near NUS · 06:00–04:00

Thirty years of student suppers; the rice arrives genuinely hot.

Selera Rasa Nasi Lemak

Adam Road Food Centre · Stall 01-02 · closed Fridays

Patronised by the Sultan of Brunei; the fried chicken is the argument.
Open-air hawker Fried, not curried Sat–Sun 7am

The most-decorated Malay nasi lemak stall in Singapore, open 7am to 3pm on both Saturday and Sunday and closed Fridays. Listings were still current in July 2026. The queue reflects the reputation — join it before 8:30 or plan for forty minutes. Crisp-skinned chicken, sambal with real depth, rice you can taste the pandan in.

vs. Sunset RailwayThe one entry that beats the benchmark outright on the food itself. Better rice, better chicken, better sambal, lower price. It costs you the queue, the seat and the aircon — Sunset Railway’s whole advantage is a table without a fight.

What changed in this update

Every entry was re-checked against current listings in August 2026. Three did not survive the audit unchanged.

Straits Club has ceased operations. The Bukit Merah Lane 1 shop that sat at number three in the last version of this guide is gone, and it has been removed rather than replaced in place.

The Coconut Club’s Ann Siang Hill shophouse has closed. The restaurant itself is very much alive — it moved to 269 Beach Road, into a larger room — so the entry survives with a corrected address.

Uptown Nasi Lemak at Telok Ayer now trades weekdays only. That fails the weekend rule outright, so this guide points at the City Gate branch on Beach Road instead, which opens all seven days.

Joining the list: Kitchenman Nasi Lemak at CT Hub, a Michelin Bib Gourmand kitchen with rendang chicken and ayam kunyit among its sides.

How the list was made

Four bars: somewhere in Singapore’s west or centre, a drive of fifteen minutes or less from Redhill Close, credible editorial coverage, and confirmed weekend trading. Ranking then favours an indoor room and a chicken curry actually on the menu. Where a stall’s curry could not be confirmed from published sources, the card says so rather than guessing.

Weekend at a glance

Open both days: Bibik Violet, The Coconut Club, Uptown at City Gate, Faith, Nusa & Tara, Super Shiok, Fong Seng, Selera Rasa. Saturday only: Kitchenman and Mr & Mrs, both closed Sundays.

Standing caveats: Selera Rasa closes Fridays, Super Shiok closes Mondays, and The Coconut Club closes the first Monday of each month.
