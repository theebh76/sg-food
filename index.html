Ten plates of nasi lemak worth leaving home for.

A dedicationMade specially for the three craftlets — may every one of these plates be worth the drive.

Ten nasi lemak plates in Singapore’s west and centre, each one checked against its Google Maps listing on 10 August 2026 — still trading, with its real rating rather than a borrowed one. Ranked by that rating, then by an indoor room and a chicken curry worth ordering. Measured against one reference plate, Cafe Sunset Railway.

◆The reference plate

Cafe Sunset Railway

41 Sunset Way · #01-02 Clementi Arcade · Thu–Sun only

Coconut basmati rice, chicken curry on the menu, a plate that arrives on ceramic.
Indoor cafe Chicken curry ✓ Sat–Sun 8am ✓

Checked on Google Maps and trading: 4.3 from 197 reviews, the newest three weeks old, and “curry chicken” among its most-mentioned dishes. Four days a week, 8am to 3pm. The counterpoint on this list — not a hawker stall but a small, quiet neighbourhood cafe that has become the yardstick for a certain kind of considered, sit-down nasi lemak. Basmati grain, chicken curry listed alongside the rice, and a room you can sit in out of the heat. It happens to satisfy both of the newer preferences, which is part of why it works as a reference. Every other entry is measured against it.

01Dhoby Ghaut

Bibik Violet

Temasek Shophouse · 28 Orchard Road · #01-05

Chicken curry at $14.90, in an air-conditioned room, seven days a week.
Indoor · aircon Chicken curry ✓ 4.6 — highest on this list

The best-rated entry here and the only one clearing every preference at once: an indoor room, a chicken curry listed in its own right, and open all seven days. Its 4.6 from 700 reviews is also the most solid number on this page. Weekday breakfast starts at 8am; weekends open at 10am, so treat it as a late brunch. Ignore the newer Great World branch on rating — it sits at 3.7.

vs. Sunset RailwayThe same pairing — nasi lemak and chicken curry in a cafe setting — but seven days against the benchmark’s four, and a third of a star higher on Google. Sunset Railway keeps the quieter room.

02Alexandra Village

Selera Tokelen

Alexandra Village Food Centre · 120A Bukit Merah Lane 1

A 4.9 average, an old-school chicken wing, and a friendly uncle behind the counter.
Open-air hawker Rendang on the board 4.9 — top rated

New to this guide and the highest-rated nasi lemak within reach of Redhill Close: 4.9 across 15 reviews, the most recent three weeks old. A proper Malay stall — nasi lemak alongside nasi padang, lontong, mee rebus and mee soto — five minutes down the road. The reviews single out the chicken wings and the rendang. Small sample size is the caveat; fifteen reviews is not seven hundred.

vs. Sunset RailwayBeats the benchmark on rating by six-tenths of a star and on price by a factor of three. Gives up the room entirely — this is a food-centre counter, not somewhere to sit over coffee.

04Kallang · CT Hub

Kitchenman Nasi Lemak

2 Kallang Avenue · #01-08/13 CT Hub · closed Sundays

A Michelin Bib Gourmand plate with rendang chicken and ayam kunyit on the sides list.
Indoor shop unit Rendang & kunyit chicken Saturday only

A Malaysian-run shop that opened in 2024 and took a Michelin Bib Gourmand for straightforward, properly made nasi lemak. Rice cooked in coconut milk with pandan, and a sides list running to ayam berempah, rendang chicken and turmeric-fried ayam kunyit. No pork or lard, though not halal-certified. Opens 11:30am, and closed Sundays.

vs. Sunset RailwayThe closest like-for-like challenger: a small kitchen, an indoor room, a curried chicken option and a critic’s badge. Sunset Railway keeps the edge on Sunday opening.

Faith Nasi Lemak

Redhill Food Centre · 85 Redhill Lane · #01-30

Crispy wings and fried bee hoon — the breakfast stall on your own doorstep.
Open-air hawker Fried, not curried Seven days

The nearest entry by a wide margin and the only one open all seven days without qualification. Trades 6am to 1:30pm, then again from 5:30pm, and runs 6am to 2pm on Sunday. Photos on its listing are three days old, so it is unambiguously alive. The crispy chicken wing is what the queue is for.

An early, halal-certified plate in the prettiest market building in town.

Fong Seng Nasi Lemak

22 Clementi Road · near NUS · 06:00–04:00

Thirty years of student suppers; the rice arrives genuinely hot.
Open-fronted coffeeshop Curry available as a side Effectively 22 hours

Open from 6am to 4am the next morning, every day, which makes it the most forgiving entry here — there is no hour of a weekend at which it is shut. Sets from about $4.50 with a long sides menu that usually includes a curry. The rating is ordinary at 4.1, but it is drawn from over thirteen hundred reviews, which makes it one of the more trustworthy numbers on this page.

vs. Sunset RailwayThe anti-Sunset-Railway: no closing days, no waiting for Thursday, a third of the price. What it gives up is the room — fluorescent-lit and functional.

Most famous

Three entries were permanently closed

Checking each listing on Google Maps rather than on food blogs turned up three that had shut. Blog coverage lags closures badly, which is how they survived the last two rounds of this guide.

Mr & Mrs Nasi Lemak on Cantonment Road is marked permanently closed. Uptown Nasi Lemak is gone entirely — both the Telok Ayer original and the City Gate branch this guide switched to last time are permanently closed. Nusa & Tara at ABC Brickworks is also closed, most likely one of the stalls that did not return after the food centre’s renovation.

Replacing them: Selera Tokelen and 106 Nasi Lemak, both in Alexandra Village Food Centre about five minutes away, and Guan Taste at Ghim Moh, reinstated now that its 4.5 rating is visible. Two strong candidates were rejected on checking — Aziema Muslim Stall at 4.8 and Hjh Maimunah Mini at 4.7 both close on Saturday and Sunday.

The ratings were wrong

Earlier versions of this guide carried ratings drawn from write-ups rather than from Google, and they were consistently too generous. The real figures: Selera Rasa is 4.0, not 4.5. The Coconut Club is 4.2, not 4.5. Fong Seng is 4.1, not 4.6. Super Shiok is 4.4, and Google has flagged that suspicious reviews were removed from its listing.

Against the original brief — above 4.5 on Google — only three entries genuinely clear the bar: Selera Tokelen at 4.9, Bibik Violet at 4.6 and Guan Taste at 4.5. The rest are kept on reputation, proximity and opening hours, with their true scores shown.

Branches

Five entries run more than one outlet, and each of those cards lists every branch inside a twenty-minute drive with its own rating and hours. In three cases the nearer branch is the weaker one: Bibik Violet at Great World is 3.7 against 4.6 at Orchard, Fong Seng at Pasir Panjang is 3.8 against 4.1 at Clementi, and The Coconut Club at New Bahru is 4.0 against 4.2 at Beach Road.

Weekend at a glance

Open both days: Bibik Violet, Faith, Super Shiok, The Coconut Club, Fong Seng, Selera Rasa. Saturday only: Kitchenman. Hours not published on Google: Selera Tokelen, Guan Taste and 106 Nasi Lemak — all three were trading when checked, but confirm before you drive.
